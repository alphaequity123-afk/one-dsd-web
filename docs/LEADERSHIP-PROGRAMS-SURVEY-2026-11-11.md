# Leadership development programs: survey of free and public models

November 11, 2026. Internal program and implementation guidance. This document is not staff-facing copy.

**Request.** The owner asked for research into other leadership development programs, including free ones, so that equity and inclusion can be set over leadership development as its governing framework, consistent with One DHS and One DSD People, Access and Culture.

**How to read this.** Each entry says what the program is, what is free, what its structure offers, and how confident the finding is. "Read" means the source document or page was opened. "Search summary" means the fact comes from a search-result summary and should be confirmed before it is repeated. Nothing here was enrolled in or tested. Availability and prices of outside courses change.

**Use of other people's work.** These are models to learn from and link to. The One DSD curriculum is written as original program content. No outside framework, course text, or assessment is copied into it.

The companion document, `LEADERSHIP-JOURNEY-CURRICULUM-2026-11-11.md`, turns these findings into an architecture, curriculum, and program outline. The MMB research is in `MMB-LEADERSHIP-PROGRAMS-RESEARCH-2026-11-11.md`.

---

## 1. What the survey shows

1. **Conventional leadership programs share one skeleton.** Nearly all of them move outward through the same rings: leading self, leading others, leading the organization, leading beyond it, and delivering results. Minnesota's model, the federal model, and the child welfare model all use it.
2. **Equity usually appears as one competency or one module among many.** That is the gap the owner's design fills. Only a few models put equity over everything else: MMB's competency guide (in its guiding statement), the Center for Creative Leadership's REAL framework (equity first), the National Equity Project's Leading for Equity, and the Annie E. Casey Foundation's Results Count.
3. **Free material is plentiful for general leadership skills and thin for equity-centered public-sector leadership.** A free, self-paced, equity-governed leadership curriculum for state human services staff does not appear to exist in the sources found. That is an observation from this search, not proof of absence.
4. **Disability-specific leadership learning exists in Minnesota and is free**, through the Governor's Council on Developmental Disabilities. It belongs in a DSD program.

## 2. Government and public-service models

| Program | What it is | Free? | What to learn from its structure | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **MMB Enterprise-Wide Leader Competency Model** (Minnesota) | Five competencies with in-progress, proficient, and expert behaviors, and an equity-centered guiding statement | Public PDF | The statewide frame. Equity is written into every competency's behaviors | Read in full |
| **MMB ELI, SEAL, Supervisor Core, Manager Core, SLI** | The State's program ladder | Tuition-based or required by appointment | Cohort rhythm, leader interviews, feedback, final presentations | See the MMB research document |
| **OPM leadership frameworks** (federal) | Executive Core Qualifications and the supervisory and managerial frameworks. The qualifications were revised effective October 1, 2025 | Frameworks are public; OPM courses are paid | A ladder from supervisor to manager to executive, with required learning at each transition. A structure reference only; this program takes its equity content from Minnesota sources | Search summary |
| **FEMA Emergency Management Institute, Professional Development Series** | Self-paced courses including IS-240 Leadership and Influence, IS-241 Decision Making and Problem Solving, IS-242 Effective Communication | Free, no prerequisites | Proof that government can run short, free, self-paced leadership courses with a completion record. Content is general, with no equity frame | Search summary |
| **Washington State DES, Equity and Employee Development** | State training division that places leadership development and equity support in one unit; includes a "Leading Others" series | Free to Washington executive agencies only | A state precedent for housing leadership and equity together | Search summary |
| **Other state programs** (Vermont VTLEAD, Delaware iLEAD, Connecticut and New York aspiring-leader programs) | Tiered state leadership curricula | Free to their own employees | Tiered by role, as the owner's five audiences are | Search summary |

## 3. Human services and disability models

| Program | What it is | Free? | What to learn from its structure | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **NCWWI Leadership Competency Framework** (National Child Welfare Workforce Institute) | Four domains: Leading Change, Leading in Context, Leading People, Leading for Results, each with defined competencies and a proficiency ladder. Separate Leadership Academies for supervisors and for middle managers | Framework is a public PDF. Academy access and cost were not confirmed | A human-services leadership model with separate tracks by role, and "Leading in Context" as its own domain | Search summary; framework dated 2010, updated 2011 |

Paired with the Race Equity and Inclusion Action Guide's seven steps | Guides, a one-hour video, and worksheets are public. The fellowships are not | Ties every leadership skill to a measurable result for a population, with disparities named. ## 4. Equity-centered leadership frameworks

| Framework | Core idea | Free? | What to learn from it | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **REAL** (Center for Creative Leadership) | Four steps: Reveal relevant opportunities, Elevate equity, Activate diversity, Lead inclusively. Equity is placed before diversity and inclusion | Articles and a 2023 research paper are public | Start with discovery in one's own context instead of importing another organization's initiative | Search summary |
| **Leading for Equity** (National Equity Project) | A stance of See, Engage, Act, supported by three disciplines: equity, complexity, design | One-page framework is public; the six-week course is paid | Leadership as an inside-out practice: how a leader sees shapes how they engage and act | Search summary |
| **Racial Equity Toolkit** (Government Alliance on Race and Equity) | Six steps for applying an equity tool to a government decision; a strategy of normalize, organize, operationalize | Public PDF | "Operationalize" is the same word this program already uses. The State of Minnesota Equity Analysis Tool remains this program's official source | Search summary |
| **Six signature traits of inclusive leadership** (Deloitte) | Commitment, courage, cognizance of bias, curiosity, cultural intelligence, collaboration | Public article; the assessment is a paid product | A plain vocabulary for inclusive leader behavior | Search summary |
| **70-20-10** (from Center for Creative Leadership research) | Most development comes from experience, some from relationships, a little from formal learning | Public | The owner's draft already uses it. The percentages are a rule of thumb and have been criticized as lacking firm evidence | Search summary |
| **IDI continuum** | Denial, Polarization, Minimization, Acceptance, Adaptation, Integration | The assessment is licensed | Already the program's theory of change. Assessment stays arm's-length | Program directive |
